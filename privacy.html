KeptClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: everything you copy stays on your Mac. KeptClip has no servers, no analytics, and no tracking. We cannot see your data because it is never sent anywhere.

What KeptClip Stores

KeptClip is a clipboard history manager. To work, it must store what you copy — text, images, and file references. This data is stored only in the app's local storage on your Mac (in the app container under ~/Library/Application Support).

What We Collect

Nothing. KeptClip:

  • Does not upload any data to any server
  • Does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs
  • Does not collect, transmit, or share personal information
  • Performs all processing — including sensitive-content detection — entirely on your device

Data Retention

  • The free version keeps your most recent 50 clips (pinned clips are kept).
  • The Pro version removes the fixed item limit. Image storage is capped locally (256 MB, oldest first).
  • You can delete individual clips at any time, or clear all history in Settings → Storage.

Deleting Your Data

  • Individual items: hover a clip and click ✕, or press ⌘⌫, or right-click → Delete.
  • Everything: Settings → Storage → Clear history (pinned clips are kept), then delete them manually if desired.
  • Complete removal: delete the KeptClip app — all data lives inside the app container and is removed with it.

Sensitive-Content Protection

KeptClip automatically detects content that looks like credit card numbers, private keys, API tokens, or national ID numbers, and refuses to store it. This detection runs entirely on your Mac using pattern matching. Nothing about detected content is transmitted or logged anywhere.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details, or any identifying information. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Permissions

KeptClip does not require Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Full Disk Access, or Screen Recording permissions. It reads the system clipboard the same way pressing ⌘V does.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: keptclip@163.com

KeptClip 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

一句话版本：你复制的所有内容都只保存在你的 Mac 上。KeptClip 没有服务器、没有统计 SDK、没有任何追踪——数据从不离开你的设备，我们也无从看到。

KeptClip 存储什么

KeptClip 是一款剪贴板历史管理工具。为实现功能，它需要保存你复制的内容——文本、图片和文件引用。这些数据仅存储在你 Mac 上的应用本地目录中（位于 ~/Library/Application Support 的应用容器内）。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。KeptClip：

  • 不向任何服务器上传数据
  • 不含统计分析、广告和任何第三方 SDK
  • 不收集、传输或共享个人信息
  • 所有处理（包括敏感内容检测）完全在你的设备上完成

数据保留

  • 免费版保留最近 50 条历史（固定置顶的条目不受影响）。
  • Pro 版解除固定条数限制。图片存储有本地配额（256 MB，超限优先淘汰最旧）。
  • 你可以随时删除单条内容，或在 设置 → 存储 中清空全部历史。

如何删除你的数据

  • 删除单条：悬停条目点击 ✕、按 ⌘⌫，或右键菜单 → 删除。
  • 清空历史：设置 → 存储 → 清空历史（固定条目保留，可再手动删除）。
  • 彻底移除：删除 KeptClip 应用——所有数据都在应用容器内，会随之一起删除。

敏感内容保护

KeptClip 会自动检测疑似银行卡号、私钥、API Token、身份证号的内容并拒绝保存。该检测完全通过本机模式匹配完成，被检测内容的任何信息都不会被传输或记录到任何地方。

关于购买

Pro 为一次性买断，购买流程完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会收到你的姓名、支付信息或任何可识别身份的信息。Apple 如何处理购买数据请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

关于权限

KeptClip 不需要辅助功能、输入监控、完全磁盘访问或屏幕录制权限。它读取系统剪贴板的方式与按下 ⌘V 粘贴相同。

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，更新版本将在本页面发布。
