KeptClip Support

A lightweight clipboard history manager for your Mac. Everything stays on your device.

Quick start: press ⌘⇧V anywhere to open your clipboard history. Type to search, ↑↓ to move, ↩ to copy, then paste with ⌘V.

Nothing appears in my history. Why?

Make sure the app is running (check the menu bar icon). KeptClip ignores copies from password managers by default — you can review this list in Settings → Privacy → Ignore Copies from These Apps.

Why was a copied item skipped?

KeptClip refuses to store content that looks like a credit card number, private key, API token, or national ID number — even in the history, that's a risk. The menu bar icon briefly turns into a shield when this happens, and Settings → Privacy shows a count of skipped items.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free keeps your last 50 clips with full search and keyboard flow. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) removes the fixed item limit and unlocks multi-select merge paste, inline preview, and app lock (Touch ID / password).

Does KeptClip require special permissions?

No. KeptClip works without Accessibility, Input Monitoring, or Full Disk Access. It reads the clipboard like any paste operation does, and nothing is ever uploaded.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single clips with ⌘⌫ or the hover ✕ button; clear everything in Settings → Storage. Deleting the app removes all data, since everything is stored locally.

Can other apps trigger KeptClip?

Yes — via URL scheme (keptclip://show, keptclip://paste?index=3, keptclip://capture) and App Intents (Shortcuts / Spotlight).

KeptClip 支持页

一款轻量的 Mac 剪贴板历史管理工具。所有内容都只保存在你的设备上。

快速上手：在任意应用中按 ⌘⇧V 呼出剪贴板历史。打字即搜索，↑↓ 移动选中，↩ 复制，然后到目标位置按 ⌘V 粘贴。

为什么历史里没有内容？

先确认应用在运行（看菜单栏图标）。KeptClip 默认忽略来自密码管理器的复制——可以在 设置 → 隐私 → 忽略以下应用的复制 中查看和调整这份列表。

为什么我复制的内容被跳过了？

KeptClip 会拒绝保存疑似银行卡号、私钥、API Token、身份证号的内容——这类信息留在历史里本身就是风险。命中时菜单栏图标会短暂变成盾牌，设置 → 隐私 中也会显示已跳过的条数。

免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？

免费版保留最近 50 条历史，搜索和键盘流完整可用。Pro（一次性买断，无订阅）解除固定条数限制，并解锁多选合并粘贴、内嵌预览和应用锁（触控 ID / 密码）。

KeptClip 需要特殊权限吗？

不需要。KeptClip 无需辅助功能、输入监控或完全磁盘访问权限。它读取剪贴板的方式和普通粘贴相同，且任何内容都不会上传。

如何删除我的数据？

单条删除用 ⌘⌫ 或悬停 ✕ 按钮；全部清空在 设置 → 存储。删除应用即可彻底移除所有数据——所有内容都保存在本地。

其他工具能调用 KeptClip 吗？

可以——通过 URL Scheme（keptclip://show、keptclip://paste?index=3、keptclip://capture）和 App Intents（快捷指令 / 聚焦搜索）。
